Pipeline

What happens between "cron fires" and "video live"

01

Pick

A niche is chosen automatically, weighted by how past videos in each one actually performed — not a fixed rotation.

02

Write & voice

An LLM writes a short hook-driven script grounded in that niche's proven patterns, then a synthesized voice reads it.

03

Edit

Captions, stock footage, and a music bed are cut together around the voice track and timed to the words.

04

Publish

The finished vertical video goes out to YouTube Shorts and TikTok automatically, no manual review step.

05

Learn

View and engagement numbers feed back into step 01, so tomorrow's niche pick gets a little smarter.

Questions

FAQ

Does a person write or approve each video?

No. Script, voice, edit, and publish all run unattended on a fixed schedule. A niche-performance history grounds which niche gets picked, but nothing is hand-reviewed before it posts.

How often does a new video go up?

Twice a day, at fixed times, with no manual step in between generation and publish.

Is this an official app or a personal project?

VaultMind is an independent personal project. It uses YouTube's and TikTok's official publishing APIs to post to its own accounts.

Where does the footage and voice come from?

Licensed stock footage and a synthesized voice track — nobody is filmed and nothing is a real person's likeness.
